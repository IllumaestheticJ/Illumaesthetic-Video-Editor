## Where Revuelto v5 agrees with them
Silver on black, whole-car opener, no turntables, once around the car front -> side -> rear, reassembly, fade, logo.
Hard cuts on bar lines (as A90 v3 plan and GR86 v5). Earliest hook of the set (3.4 s). Wide removal then close on the
removed-state scan. Bumper sub-parts explode only after the bumper is off (400Z rule). Lid hinges only, because only a
Hood Open scan exists. Music from the song's start (ND rule) at about -14 LUFS (400Z). Large logo (later reels all
moved to 640-860 px). Scanned plate kept (blur is IS300-only). No wheel close-up (ND).

## Where Revuelto v5 differs - carry these into the next reel
- **Side section is the weak one.** ND: "the side skirt comes off and then immediately cuts away, its just a bad
  practice". Revuelto's side shot ends ~0.9 s after the last part parks, has no close shot of the fender-removed /
  skirts-removed state scans and no label. Give the side the same wide -> close -> label treatment as the ends.
- **Not every part is named.** GR86: "make sure all parts are highlighted". Fender, skirts and fin have no label;
  headlights and tail lights are only sub-lines.
- **Scan specks remain** under the open front lid. A90: "scan artifacts must be cut out".
- **Parts translate, they do not articulate.** A90 asks for "excruciating detail" in how parts articulate; only the
  lid hinges here.
- **No underbody or interior.** A90: "underbody and interior must be in the reel too". None listed among the
  Revuelto scans, so this is a data limit - check Drive before assuming so on the next car.
- **Length.** 44.6 s is the longest of the set; the style guide says 15-30 s and GR86 was told to "move faster".
  The reviewer accepted it here because every added second was a section they asked for - do not pad to reach it.
- **Label wording.** NC: "label what the scan is used for". Revuelto labels name parts and states plus three
  triangle counts.
- **No drop under the reassembly** (a consequence of starting at the song's beginning).
