# Del & Eps: the Epsilon+ characters

A father and son who explain our work in videos and posts: the process (scan to shelf), prototypes (cut testing),
kit installs, and the FAQ. They live in our 3D scan data: the cars and parts stay plain silver scans, and the
characters carry the colour.

Tagline: **"For every ε, there's a δ."** The names come from the epsilon–delta definition of a limit. Eps (ε) is
the small detail that makes the difference (the "+" in Epsilon+). Del (δ) is the tolerance that gets you there.

## The locked look (2026-11-17)

Simplified, matte arcade-racing-game 3D style: smooth plastic-like surfaces, minimal faces, slightly big heads,
mitten-like gloves, chunky boots, pit radio headsets. Reference: `del-eps-model-sheet.jpg`. Use it as the
reference image for every new image or video so the two stay consistent.

| | Del (δ), the dad | Eps (ε), the son |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Fabricator, fit and finish. Explains **why**. | Runs the scanner and CAD. Asks the customer's questions, does the install. |
| Build | Tall, broad, middle-aged | Slimmer, younger |

| Props | Yellow masking-tape roll on his belt, drill template, the 10 mm socket | Handheld 3D scanner (dark pistol grip, rounded head, two cyan lenses), cordless drill |
| Voice | Low, slow, short sentences | Bright, fast |
| Lines | "Measure twice, drill once." · "Template first." · "That's a δ problem." | "Okay but what if we scan it?" · "Customer asks…" · "Where's my 10 mm?" |

Colours: Del `#0f5c63` petrol teal, `#ff7a1a` signal orange. Eps `#6b3cff` violet, `#ffd21f` hi-vis yellow.
Scanner light / drill marks `#6fc3d6` cyan. Scans stay silver on black.

Running gag: Eps loses his 10 mm socket; Del always has it.

## Rules for the scan world

- Cars and parts are our real scans in the house silver; the characters are the only colour in frame.
- 1:1 scale next to the car for installs; about 1:10 "micro mode" standing on a part for close-ups and bolt locations.
- Parts come off the way they unbolt, and nothing is shown removed unless we have a scan of it removed.
- Never put made-up specs or numbers on screen.

## Series

- **Scan to Shelf** (Del leads): the work process, one car per episode.
- **Cut Test** (Eps leads): prototypes; v1 rarely fits.
- **Bolt-On** (Eps does, Del watches): install guides on the car's own scan.
- **Ask Eps** (10–20 s): one FAQ per short.
