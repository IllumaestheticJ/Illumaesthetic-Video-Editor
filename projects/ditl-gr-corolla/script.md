# DITL: "Day in the life of an average GR Corolla owner": script v2, 2026-11-19

**Status: owner answered questions 1-4 on 2026-11-19 (see "Owner decisions"); first cut being built. Credits spent: 0.92 (three voice lines).**

Owner's request (2026-11-19): "this day in the life should show all the stereotypes of gr corolla owners / feature
their main competitors the type r and wrx / create a script using these tropes and stereotypes. / show how the
reason they got the Gr corolla was to be 'different'".

**Runtime:** about 33 s. **14 shots**, most 1.6 s, hard cuts on the song's 4-beat grid (grid is fixed after the
song's BPM and first hit are measured).
**Frame:** 1080x1920; game picture 1080x1080 at y = 420-1499; title in the top band on two lines:
`Day in the life of an` / `average GR Corolla owner`. No Epsilon+ branding, no end card, hard cut to black.
**Format:** as approved on video #1 (WRX): all Blender, simulator-game look with game textures and heavy crunch,
POV hands always busy, flat-faced low-poly people with swapped face decals, minimal voices, wall-to-wall SFX.

## The character
He bought the GR Corolla to be "different" from the Type R guys and the WRX guys. He says "it's not a Corolla"
daily. He paid a dealer markup. He brags about three cylinders. He plays with the AWD dial in car parks. Everyone
else (his grandma, a rideshare passenger, the guy at the pump) calls it a Corolla.

The running gag is one joke that escalates: he says "different", the world says "Corolla".
