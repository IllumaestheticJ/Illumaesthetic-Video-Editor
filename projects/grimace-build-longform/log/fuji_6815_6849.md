## 1. Install coverage

- **Strut tower brace (Whiteline)**: best-covered item in this batch, on Grimace itself. Fitting: 6817 (close, impact on the strut-top nut, #558 visible) and 6819 (wide of the same step). Finished: 6820 (whole bay, brace installed), 6818 (bracket on tower, short), 6821 (macro of the Whiteline logo on the bar). Missing: unboxing, bare tower before the brace, any torque-wrench step (only an impact gun is shown).
- **Bushings**: 6816 shows one clear fitting step, steel sleeve pressed into a bushing in a red control arm with the bench vise, ending with the sleeve flush (25 s). 6815 is the same job from a poor low angle (arm and tool visible 0-5 s and 44-47 s). Not shown: removal of old bushings, pressing the bushing itself into the arm, grease, the arm going back on the car. Which arm and which corner cannot be told; bushing brand not visible in these clips.
- **Coilovers**: none for Grimace. (6846 shows coilovers in a box, but they belong to the other #24 car.)
- **Big brake kit, pads/fluid, sway bars, fender braces, tie rods, steering rack bushings, steering wheel**: none in this range.
- **Other**:
  - Grimace parked in the shop, static background: 6822 (wide).
  - Oil drain on a car on the lift: 6823, 6824 (car unconfirmed, most likely the Celica seen on the lift in 6822; not a parts-install step).
  - No before/after (old part next to new part) and no torque or measurement step anywhere in this range.
