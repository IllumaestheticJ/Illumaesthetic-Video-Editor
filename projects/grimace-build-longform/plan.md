# Grimace parts-build long-form: director's plan (v1, for owner approval)

Sources: `coverage.md`, `clips.md`, `log/*.md` (clip names and spans below are from the logs, in seconds from clip start),
the long-form skill, the bracing video's script and the owner's 22 notes on it. Nothing here is cut yet.
Clip shorthand: `6662` = `DSCF6662`; `5438` = `049A5438`; `Jun07 2_04_58 PM` = `Video_Jun_07_2026_2_04_58_PM`.
Every explainer line is worded as how that kind of part works, never as a measured result on Grimace.
Anything marked **ASK** needs the owner before it goes on screen or into the voice.

## 1. Positioning

**Promise (one sentence):** We took our purple Miata race car off cheap eBay coilovers, threw a whole table of real race
parts at it, and this is what every part is, what it does, and what happened when we took it to Sonoma.

**Who it is for:** Miata and budget track-car owners deciding what to buy next (coilovers, bushings, brakes, bracing,
steering), plus the followers of the bracing video who already know Grimace.

**Why they keep watching:** (1) a visible checklist of parts that fills in chapter by chapter, (2) one "what does it
actually do" animation per part, short enough to finish, (3) the track payoff promised in the first 30 seconds and held
to the end, (4) the shop humour, which is real and already on tape.

### Shapes considered
| Shape | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| (a) One video now: table + graphics + thin installs | Fastest. Spine and jokes exist | Five of ten sponsors would be a box on a table plus an animation. No proof the parts are on the car. No payoff |

**Recommendation: ship (a), gated on two cheap things, not on re-filming installs.**
1. The owner uploads the on-track shots and conclusions they already have.
2. One short pick-up session with the car as it is today (about one hour, car on the lift, wheels off): finished-state
   close-ups of every part on the car plus four short lines to camera (section 7, "must"). The parts are already
   fitted, so this is filming, not wrenching, and it is the single thing that turns the under-served sponsors into
   properly served ones.

Minimum for the video to be good: items M1-M6 and O1-O3 in section 7. If the pick-up session cannot happen within about
two weeks, ship shape (c) at about 7 minutes under title 3 (drop chapter 8, shorten chapters 4 and 6, end on the parts
map and "next: the install and the track"), and say so plainly in the voice.

Work we can do now without the owner: re-fetch the 4K originals of 6662 150-197 and 5438 14-26 to try to read the box
labels and the wheel brand; pull and log the 80 GB GoPro `On-Track Dump` one file at a time (it has never been viewed and

## 3. Structure

Target runtime **about 12:15**. Narration = owner's cloned voice unless marked SYNC (real on-camera speech). Music runs
continuously at one level through every section, including under graphics. Every chapter opens with a title card and
the parts-map sting (G3) and carries a supplier lower-third (G4). Credit types: **S** spoken, **L** lower-third, **C** logo card.

| # | Section | Time | Dur |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Cold open | 0:00-0:30 | 30 s |
| 1 | Title + intro | 0:30-1:25 | 55 s |
| 2 | Coilovers (MCS, Eibach) | 1:25-3:05 | 100 s |
| 3 | Bushings (Whiteline; Good-Win grease and seals) | 3:05-4:50 | 105 s |
| 4 | Brakes (Fast Brakes, Wilwood, CSG) | 4:50-6:35 | 105 s |
| 5 | Bracing and sway bars (Whiteline, Good-Win) | 6:35-7:55 | 80 s |
| 6 | Steering (SPL, SuperPro, the wheel from Mike Kojima) | 7:55-9:40 | 105 s |

| 8 | Track payoff: Sonoma | 10:00-11:10 | 70 s |
| 9 | Conclusions | 11:10-11:45 | 35 s |
| 10 | Sign-off | 11:45-12:15 | 30 s |

**Why this order.** Coilovers first: the most wanted part, and the only one with a real before/after on tape (the eBay
unit in the host's hand). Bushings second: the best real action in the dump, so the viewer gets a proper job early.
Brakes third: high interest, strongest beauty shots, and by then the viewer will accept an animation-led chapter.
Bracing fourth and short: it gives the audience of the previous video a callback and a link without re-running it.
Steering last: it is the funniest, the host is on camera, and the wheel is the one part we can show in use at the
track, so it cuts straight into Sonoma. Real action and graphics alternate so no two graphic-heavy stretches touch.
