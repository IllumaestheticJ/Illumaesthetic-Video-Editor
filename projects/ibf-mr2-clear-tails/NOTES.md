So the AFTER section uses the store photos, and there is **no night section yet**.

**Please film (phone, 4K, vertical 9:16 if possible):**
1. **Same car before/after, daytime.** A low rear 3/4 of the tail light, camera about 50 cm from the lens at lens height, 5-8 s of slow push-in. Film it once with the stock lens and once with the clear lens, from the same spot (mark it with tape). This gives the 1:1 before/after cut.
2. **Full rear, daytime.** Straight behind at bumper height, about 3 m back, both tails in frame, 5 s still + 5 s slow walk-in. Again once stock and once clear.

4. **Optional:** the lens being fitted (old lens off, clear lens on, 10-15 s handheld close-up), and the lens pair on a dark surface, rotated by hand (5 s).
