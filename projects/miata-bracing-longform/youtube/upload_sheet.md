## Description (paste as is)

Everyone says chassis bracing makes a difference. We wanted a number. So we borrowed a stock NB Miata, jacked up one front corner to twist the chassis, and 3D scanned it five times with an Artec Leo: on the ground, jacked with no bracing, with fender braces, with a strut tower brace, and with both.

What we measured (engine bay width between the strut towers, one corner jacked):
• No bracing: 1.7 mm wider than the car on the ground
• Fender braces only: 0.3 mm
• Strut tower brace only: 0.8 mm
• Both: 0.5 mm

One car, one scan per setup, and a jack instead of a real corner, so treat this as a first look, not the final word. Both braces worked. Ranking them needs more controlled testing.
