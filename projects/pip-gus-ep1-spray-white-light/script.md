## The science (qualitative only, no numbers)
- The scanner projects a pattern of light onto the surface; a camera sees how the pattern bends over the shape.
- Projector and camera sit a known distance apart, so the angle tells it where each point is (triangulation).
- It needs the surface to scatter the light back. Glass lets it through, chrome mirrors it away, black paint absorbs it.
- A thin matte coat makes every surface scatter the light the same way.
- The scanner is never named. No brand of spray is named in Ep 1 (Ep 9 covers AESUB).
