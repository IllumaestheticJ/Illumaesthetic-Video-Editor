## The science (qualitative only, no numbers)
- The scanner projects a pattern of light; a camera sees how the pattern bends over the shape.
- Projector and camera sit a fixed distance apart, so the angle tells it where each point is (triangulation).
- It needs the surface to scatter light back. Glass lets it through, chrome mirrors it away, black absorbs it.
- A thin matte coat on just those parts makes them scatter like the rest of the car.
- (Optional) Big smooth panels all look alike to the scanner; a sprayed pattern gives it something to track.
