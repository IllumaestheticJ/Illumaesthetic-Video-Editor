# Pip & Gus Ep 2: "Can't you just 3D print the scan?" (script v1, for owner approval)

9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps, **about 34-35 s**. Concept approved by the owner. This version is tightened from
`projects/pip-gus-series/scripts.md` (Ep 2). Nothing gets voiced or rendered until the owner approves it.

**Question the episode answers:** "I bought the scan, so why can't I just print a part from it?"
**Answer in one line:** a scan is a skin (a surface of tiny triangles with no thickness). It's the *reference*: you design
the part on it, and the scan is what makes the part fit.

Changes from the series draft (and why):
- Dropped "No edges." It's vague and easy to argue with; "Tiny triangles. No thickness." is the accurate core claim.
- Lines 6-7 use the series "Is it done yet?" gag but flip the answer to "...Yep." The punchline is that it *is* done, and it's useless.
  This keeps the series gag fresh without adding a line.
- Lines 7 and 8 split the flop from the explanation, so the visual joke gets its own beat.
- New hand-off (lines 11-12), varied per the series rule: Pip finds Illumaesthetic's scans on the phone, and Gus's "Of course they do"
  keeps him dry, not angry. The hand-off leads straight into the scan-sales CTA on the logo card.
- No numbers anywhere in the cartoon (no triangle count, no sizes, no times).

Illumaesthetic logo (about 720 px wide on 1080) + **SCANS AVAILABLE AT ILLUMAESTHETIC.COM**. Pip's line 11 sets this up, so the
CTA is the episode's payoff. Owner rule (2026-11-16): a CTA is optional and used only when relevant. It is relevant here because the
episode is about buying and using a scan. If the owner prefers, the logo card stands alone and line 11 still works without it.

No "FULL VIDEO ON OUR CHANNEL" teaser card. Owner rule (2026-11-16, lessons.md): videos don't need to link to a YouTube video, so
the card is optional and used only when a matching full video exists. Ep 2's natural CTA is buying a scan. If the owner names a
matching full video, the pilot's card can go back in before the logo (+3 s).
