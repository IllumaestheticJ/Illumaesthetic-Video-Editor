## Changes from the series draft (and why)
- Hook rebuilt as a 3-beat gag (vent -> "Park it in the sun" -> "It's melting!") so the problem lands in the first ~5 s.
- Added Pip's "So what do I use?" so the material list answers a question instead of being a lecture.
- Each material line now starts with *where it goes* (inside / outside / brackets / bends): a viewer can remember four places more easily than five names.
- Dropped the PETG "dull look over time" picture: it implies a claim we haven't sourced.
- "Nylon and carbon-fiber blends" became one material ("carbon-fiber nylon") to save ~1.5 s; the owner picks the exact blend (Q1).
- "And the hard ones to print?" became "Why doesn't everyone just print those?" - a more natural set-up for the enclosure line.
- Added the failed-prints shelf gag and a phone hand-off, per the series format.

## Rules this script follows
- No temperatures, numbers, prices or print times, on screen or spoken. All material traits are general and qualitative:
  PLA has low heat resistance; PETG is tougher and more heat-tolerant than PLA; ASA is made for UV and weather;
  ASA (and ABS) tend to warp and are printed in an enclosure; carbon-fiber nylon is stiff and strong; TPU is flexible.
- No filament, printer or competitor brand is named or shown.
- No text over the real footage; captions are on the cartoon only.
