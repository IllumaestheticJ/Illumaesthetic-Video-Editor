## Owner answers (2026-11-16)
- **Methods used:** superglue, epoxy, welding and staples. Which one depends on the application and what the part is for.
- **Solvent welding:** yes, acetone, on ASA/ABS only.
- **Alignment:** no pins, keys or dovetails. Illumaesthetic parts are too thin, and dovetails don't really help. This was cut from the script.
- **Which is best:** superglue for mold masters. Staples and welding when the part needs to be strong.
- **Footage:** timelapses exist on the Dropbox phone backup.
