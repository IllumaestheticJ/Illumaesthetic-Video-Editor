# "The usual way" - scan-tedium explainer reel: script (for owner approval)

9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps, ~34 s. **Part A** (0-15.5 s): animated block characters in a cartoon shop act out the usual way to scan a
whole car. **Part B** (15.5-34.0 s): hard cut on a beat to the real IS350 footage and the 1:1 real-to-scan cuts already rendered for the
IS350 reels, ending on the full scan and the logo card with **SCANS AVAILABLE AT ILLUMAESTHETIC.COM**.

Shot ids (S1-S10, P1-P10) are defined in `storyboard.md`. Every number on screen is SOURCED in `research.md` (claim id in brackets).

## Rules this script follows
- No scanner brand is named or mocked: "the usual way", "a lot of scanners". The IS350 scanner is not named (brands are owner-only).
- No invented numbers. On screen: "~8 CM" [S3], "4+" [S1], "3+" [A5]. No time figure for the usual way (sources disagree); the
  wall clock has ticks, no numerals, and no time is printed. Part B does **not** show 32 or 20 minutes (owner's R3 note).
- Text over real footage only as the IS350 reels already do it: small captions of spoken lines and one top-band title, in the reel safe area
  (no text in the top 220 px, bottom 420 px, right 120 px). The animated part carries text freely.
- The IRL -> scan transformation is a hard 1:1 cut on a beat (no dissolve). Part A -> Part B is also a hard cut on a beat.
- Voices are free TTS voices pitch/formant-shifted into cartoons. No real person's voice is imitated (see Voices).

## Characters
- **PIP** - small yellow cuboid, big eyes, impatient (the customer). High, fast voice.
- **GUS** - tall orange cuboid, slow, holds the handheld scanner (does the work). Low, dopey voice.
- **SCANNER** (version B only) - the handheld scanner beeps a robot line. Squeaky voice + bitcrush.

Reading: with the cast as written (PIP = squeaky or goofy, GUS = dopey) every line a character actually speaks was heard correctly
(A1 PIP 0.00, A2 GUS 0.00, B1 PIP goofy 0.00 / squeaky 0.25, B2 GUS 0.20 = only the interjection "Ha" missed). Squeaky at +6 st loses
plosive endings on one-word shouts ("Quick" -> "Click"); goofy at +3 st blurs vowels in "dots" -> "dats". Recommended cast: **PIP = squeaky**
(with "Hurry" in place of "Quick"), **GUS = dopey**, **SCANNER = goofy + bitcrush**. Piper is stochastic: the final build should render 3-5
takes per line and keep the one that transcribes cleanly.
